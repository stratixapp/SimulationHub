Service Details

About the Service

▾

This service allows a Registrant to file their VAT Return (VAT201) for a specific tax period, declaring the VAT due on sales and other outputs, and VAT recoverable on expenses and other inputs. In this simulator, every figure you see in the return is one you will type in yourself, exactly as a real registrant would.

Eligibility Criteria

▾

Any VAT-registered taxable person with an active TRN and an open filing period may submit a VAT201 return for that period.

Your service journey

▾

VAT Return details → VAT on Sales & Outputs → Imports → VAT on Expenses & Inputs → Net VAT Calculation → Additional Reporting → Review & Declaration → Submission.

FAQ

▾

This is a training simulator. No submission here is transmitted to the Federal Tax Authority or any government system.
