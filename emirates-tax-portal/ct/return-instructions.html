Required Documents

List of documents that are required for filing the Corporate Tax Return is provided below (where applicable):

  • Financial statements
  • Documentation supporting the market value of Qualifying Immovable Property at the start of the first tax period
  • Documentation supporting the market value of financial assets and liabilities at the start of the first tax period
  • Evidence of foreign tax paid and tax residency of the foreign country, where a foreign tax credit is claimed

Accepted file types are PDF, DOC and DOCX. The individual file size limit is 15 MB.

Service Details
About the Service

Use this service to submit the Corporate Tax Return for the applicable tax period. Amounts are entered to the nearest Dirham; decimal values cannot be entered in amount fields.

For guidance on completing the tax return, refer to the FTA Corporate Tax Returns guide published on tax.gov.ae.

Eligibility Criteria

Every Taxable Person registered for Corporate Tax must file a return for each tax period, within nine months of the end of that tax period, even where no tax is payable.

Service Steps
  1. Confirm the taxpayer details and the taxable person information.
  2. Make the applicable elections for the tax period.
  3. Enter the accounting schedules taken from the financial statements.
  4. Record accounting adjustments and exempt income.
  5. Claim the applicable reliefs and tax losses.
  6. Record other adjustments such as non-deductible expenditure.
  7. Review the tax liability and claim tax credits.
  8. Review the return, complete the declaration and submit.
FAQs

Is a return required when there is no taxable income? Yes. A return must be filed for every tax period once registered.

What is the 0% threshold? The first AED 375,000 of taxable income is taxed at 0%; the balance is taxed at 9%.

Who can elect Small Business Relief? A resident taxable person whose revenue in the tax period and previous tax periods does not exceed AED 3,000,000, subject to the published conditions.
