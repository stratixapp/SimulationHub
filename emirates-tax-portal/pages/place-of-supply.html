Place of Supply & Intra-GCC - Practice Set

For each case choose where the supply takes place and the VAT treatment, then check your work.

Rules used (Federal Decree-Law 8/2017)
  • Goods: Art. 27. In the UAE if supplied in the UAE with no export or import. Exports to places outside the Applying States are treated as supplied in the UAE and are zero rated (Art. 45(1)).
  • Services: Art. 29 default is the supplier's place of residence. Art. 30 special cases: recipient registered in an Applying State; business recipient in the UAE with a non-resident supplier (UAE); services related to goods; hotel and catering and similar services (where performed); transport. Art. 31 telecom and electronic services (where used). Art. 32 place of residence.
  • Reverse charge: Art. 48(1). The UAE business recipient accounts for the tax (Box 3) and recovers it if entitled (Box 10).
  • Designated Zones (FTA guide VATGDZ1, July 2018): a business in a Designated Zone is treated as resident in the UAE. Services supplied in a Designated Zone are taxed under the normal rules. A supply from the mainland into a Designated Zone is local, not an export. A movement from a Designated Zone into the mainland is an import. Sales of goods inside a Designated Zone depend on Art. 51(5) as amended in 2021 (FTA VATP027) and are not covered in this set yet.
  • Exported services: zero-rating also needs the service not to be one whose place of supply is fixed by the special cases (services related to goods, hotels, real estate, transport, telecom) in the UAE (Exec. Reg. Art. 31(1)(a)(3), since 15 Nov 2024).
  • Intra-GCC: FTA VATP019 says the UAE currently does not recognise any other state as an Implementing State, so Saudi Arabia, Oman, Bahrain, Kuwait and Qatar are treated like non-GCC states. The 2026 amendments to the GCC Unified VAT Agreement are not yet reflected in UAE law as far as the sources read show. Check the FTA before teaching this point.

Sources: Federal Decree-Law 8/2017 (UAE Legislation Portal), FTA Public Clarification VATP019 and FTA Designated Zones VAT Guide VATGDZ1. Training exercise only, not tax advice.
