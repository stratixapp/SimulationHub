About this simulator

This is an offline, front-end recreation of the UAE Federal Tax Authority's EmaraTax VAT201 return filing workflow, built for training and educational purposes only. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Federal Tax Authority in any way. No real API is called, no backend server is used, and nothing you enter ever leaves your browser — all data lives in this browser's local storage.

VAT Filing Frequency

Choose how often you're required to file. On the real portal the FTA assigns your tax periods (quarterly "stagger" group or monthly) and shows them in EmaraTax; this simulator uses plain calendar quarters or months for practice. Changing this regenerates your upcoming (Draft) filing periods — already-Submitted returns are kept as-is.

Customs & Tourist-Refund Feed (Box 2 & Box 6)

On the real VAT201, Box 2 and Box 6 are locked: they only show a figure when the tourist-refund operator or UAE Customs actually reported something for your TRN. By default this simulator behaves the same way, so both stay 0.00. Switch the training feed on to practise a Box 6 → Box 7 correction and the Box 10 recovery. Applies to open (Draft) returns only; submitted returns are never changed.
