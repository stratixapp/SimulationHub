About this simulator

This is an offline, front-end recreation of the UAE Federal Tax Authority's EmaraTax VAT201 return filing workflow, built for training and educational purposes only. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Federal Tax Authority in any way. No real API is called, no backend server is used, and nothing you enter ever leaves your browser — all data lives in this browser's local storage.
