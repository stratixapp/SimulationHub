Input VAT on Employee Expenses - from 1 October 2026

Is the input VAT recoverable for periods from 1 October 2026? Choose for each case, then check.

Rules used and how far they are verified
  • Entertainment is blocked (FTA VATP005, read in full). Free goods and services for employees are blocked unless a case in FTA Decision No. 17 of 2026 applies (Executive Regulation Art. 53(1)(c) as amended by Cabinet Decision 149 of 2026).
  • Decision 17 (issued 9 Sep 2026, effective 1 Oct 2026) lists six cases, each with conditions that must all be met and a contract or documented policy: employee transport; food and drink in remote areas; work-required accommodation; new-hire temporary accommodation up to 30 days; phones, data and home internet with a use policy and monitoring; parking for business use.
  • Verification status: the Ministry of Finance announcement confirms Cabinet Decision 149 of 2026. The text of Decision 17 and of Decision 149 could not be opened; the six cases come from firm notes (Naqood, Alvarez and Marsal, fame.ae and others agree). Check tax.gov.ae before teaching.
