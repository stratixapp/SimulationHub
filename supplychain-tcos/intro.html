About ICEGATE and this Training Replica

The real ICEGATE

ICEGATE is the national e-commerce gateway of Indian Customs, run by the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC). Importers, exporters, customs brokers and freight forwarders use it to file Bills of Entry and Shipping Bills electronically, pay duty online, upload supporting documents, reply to Customs queries and follow the status of their filings. Moving from paper to this single window is what makes customs clearance faster, traceable and paperless.

This training replica

This portal is a training replica of that workflow, designed and built by Ananthu Shaji for students of logistics and EXIM. It follows the real sequence of a customs filing: sign in, describe the shipment, fill every step of the declaration, validate, sign, pass Customs processing and download the document. It is connected to the Logistics Documentation Simulator, so the documents you prepare there become the particulars you declare here, and your Logistics ID and password work in both.

What is real, what is simulated

Simulated for training

  • DSC token, PIN, OTP and payments are simulated
  • No data reaches any government system
  • Not the official ICEGATE portal

Latest Training Advisories

Short notes that save time when you file.

19 Nov 2026Sign-in

Use your Logistics Simulator ID to sign in

If you reach this portal through "Do it in ICEGATE", your Logistics ID and password are already filled in. Type the captcha and log in. The same account details carry across both simulators.

19 Nov 2026Shipping Bill

The 21 Shipping Bill steps start blank

Nothing is auto-filled into the wizard. Open your prepared documents (invoice, packing list, shipping instructions) and type each particular yourself. Only the Export Brief is pre-filled from your job.

18 Nov 2026Clearance

PGA clearance before Let Export Order

Defence stores, aircraft / RPAS and scheduled chemicals need clearance from the relevant agency before Customs will issue the LEO. Apply from the filing screen and answer any query.

18 Nov 2026Bill of Entry

Carrier IGM and the IGM register

The Bill of Entry must match the IGM filed by the carrier. If your Bill of Lading is not on the register, simulate the carrier's filing and search again.

17 Nov 2026Valuation

How duty is computed in this portal

Assessable Value = CIF in INR plus 1% landing charges. Duty = BCD on AV, then Social Welfare Surcharge at 10% of BCD, then IGST on AV + BCD + SWS. Freight and insurance are added when the incoterm does not include them.

17 Nov 2026Security

DSC, PIN and OTP are training-only

The token PIN and OTP are shown inside the simulator so you can practise the full Submit & Sign sequence. Never enter a real DSC PIN or OTP.

Before you file: checklist

  • Commercial invoice with value, currency and incoterm
  • Packing list with package count and weights
  • Shipping instructions / booking confirmation (port, vessel, container)
  • IEC, GSTIN and PAN of the exporter or importer
  • HS code and description for every item
  • Certificates the goods or scheme need (origin, inspection, PGA)
  • A valid Class-3 DSC for the signatory (simulated)

Common mistakes

  • Gross weight smaller than net weight, or item weights that do not add up to the package total
  • Invoice value that does not equal the sum of the item lines
  • HS code that does not match the description
  • Wrong port code or country of destination
  • Signatory name or designation that differs between the declaration and the Shipping Bill
  • Skipping the supporting-document attach step before Submit
LEARN BY DOING

Video Tutorials & Guided Walkthroughs

Your trainer shares the video lessons. These written walkthroughs follow exactly the same sequence; open one to see every step.

▶Filing a Shipping BillStep-by-step walkthrough · 21 steps
  1. Prepare the commercial invoice, packing list and shipping instructions in the Logistics Documentation Simulator.
  2. Open the Shipping Bill document and choose "Do it for real, in ICEGATE".
  3. Log in (your Logistics ID is filled in) and type the captcha.
  4. Check the pre-filled Export Brief and continue to the Shipping Bill.
  5. Type the 21 steps: exporter, consignee, shipment, invoice, items, scheme details, packages, containers, supporting documents, declaration.
  6. Run Validate, fix every flagged error, then Submit & Sign.
  7. Complete checklist verification, declarations, DSC selection, token PIN and OTP, then Submit to ICES.
▶Filing a Bill of EntryStep-by-step walkthrough · 24 steps
  1. Complete the arrival documents (Bill of Lading, cargo manifest, arrival notice) in the Logistics Simulator.
  2. Choose "Do it for real, in ICEGATE" on the Bill of Entry.
  3. Verify the IGM on the IGM Verification step.
  4. Enter importer, supplier, invoice, item and valuation particulars.
  5. Attach the required supporting documents in e-Sanchit.
  6. Validate and submit, then pay the duty.
  7. Confirm CFS gate-in and wait for the Out of Charge order, then download the PDF.
▶Fixing validation errorsStep-by-step walkthrough · Troubleshooting
  1. Read the error text; it names the step and field.
  2. Use the field help to understand the rule.
  3. Compare the value with the source document.
  4. Correct it and validate again.
  5. Practise further in the Error Resolution Lab and Compliance Lab.
▶After submission: PGA to LEOStep-by-step walkthrough · Customs processing
  1. Apply for any PGA clearance the screen offers.
  2. Choose "Proceed with Customs Processing".
  3. Answer any query Customs raises.
  4. If examination finds a discrepancy, submit the corrective action.
  5. Issue the Let Export Order, then file the EGM.
  6. Download the Shipping Bill PDF; you return to the Logistics Simulator automatically.

Training Helpdesk

Help for students using the simulator.

Ask your trainer

For questions about a filing, a validation error or a lost login, ask your trainer or instructor. They can see your filings and reset practice accounts.

Report a problem

If something does not behave as described, tell your trainer the filing reference (it looks like TRN-SB-… or TRN-BOE-…), the step you were on and the message you saw. Built and maintained by Ananthu Shaji.

Frequently asked questions

I came from the Logistics Simulator. Which ID and password do I use?

The same ones you use in the Logistics Documentation Simulator. When you arrive through "Do it in ICEGATE" they are already filled in; you can also clear them and type the same ID and password by hand.

What is the captcha for?

It copies the real portal's login. Type the characters shown in the box and press Login. The refresh icon gives a new one.

Where do I find the OTP and the DSC token PIN?

In the training console. The SMS inbox on the OTP screen shows the six-digit OTP, and the PIN screen shows the training token PIN. Never type real credentials anywhere in this simulator.

Why is the Shipping Bill empty when I open it?

By design. The 21 steps start blank so you practise typing every particular from your documents. Only the Export Brief before them is pre-filled from your job.

The IGM was not found while filing a Bill of Entry.

Use "File Standalone IGM (simulate carrier filing)" on the IGM Verification step, then search the IGM register again.

Customs asked for a DGCA / Ministry of Defence / chemicals clearance. What now?

Some cargo needs a Partner Government Agency clearance before the Let Export Order. Use the "Apply for … clearance" buttons on the filing screen and reply to any query that comes back.

How do I get my PDF and go back to the Logistics Simulator?

When the filing is complete (LEO + EGM for a Shipping Bill, OOC for a Bill of Entry) a completion box appears. Press the download-and-return button; the PDF downloads and you are taken back to your document in the Logistics Simulator, now marked complete.

Is anything here real?

No. This is a training replica built by Ananthu Shaji. All parties, numbers, payments and clearances are fictional, and nothing is sent to any government system.
